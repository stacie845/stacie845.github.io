About Us

Founded in 1988, Spoke O'Motion is wholly committed to bringing cycling to our community. Our continued growth is based upon our ability to reach out to riders and improve their experience on two wheels. Whether you are aiming for a podium finish at your next race or looking to cruise down to Fairy Lake to enjoy a splash of sunshine on a summer’s day, Spoke O'Motion is there to help create the ideal experience. This encompasses your customer service when you first begin shopping, the joy you feel with each pedal stroke on your slick new ride, the consistent and reliable repair and service work our ace mechanics deliver, and the confidence that we will look after your needs in the long-term. This relationship is at the core of the Spokes experience. At our store, you are not a number or a shipping address. You are unique in your needs and deserve the utmost in customer service excellence whether you are spending $20 or $20,000. Our reputation in this regard is unmatched.
