About Us

Founded in 1988, Spoke O'Motion is wholly committed to bringing cycling to the community. Continued growth is based on the ability to reach out to riders and improve experience on two wheels. Whether aiming for a podium finish at the next race or cruising down to Fairy Lake, Spoke O'Motion provides support for an ideal cycling experience. This includes customer service when shopping, enjoyment during each pedal stroke on a new bike, consistent and reliable repair and service work, and assurance of long-term support. This relationship is at the core of the Spokes experience. Each customer is unique and receives high-quality service regardless of purchase value. Reputation in this regard is unmatched.
